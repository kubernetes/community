## What I have done

My initial experience in Kubernetes was building the prototype that would
become the Helm project. Deis were acquired by Microsoft, and tasked to
build Azure's managed Kubernetes product: AKS. I maintained the open source
footprint for that effort: aks-engine. As and engineer and manager at
Microsoft, I have been committed to driving CNCF standards (for example, DRA)
into Azure; I have worked across industry to bring innovation into the CNCF
(for example, the Karpenter project); and I have worked to expose Azure
platforms as portable standards (for example, partnering with Red Hat to
leverage CAPZ for multi-cloud services).

I currently serve as the Tech Lead and Chair of SIG Autoscaling, supporting
AI engineering efforts like DRA, and human efforts like recognizing and
promoting long-time community contributors, growing our Tech Lead footprint
by 2x, leading to a more diverse and sustainable community.

## What I'll do

My number one priority will be to optimize the Kubernetes project for balancing
the interests of entrenched stakeholder institutions with the larger
representative community of contributors and companies operating on a smaller
scale. Creating a standard infrastructure platform within a thriving ecosystem
requires both large-scale monetary investment from major for-profit institutions,
as well as large-scale time and energy investment from human-scale sources:
individuals, start-ups, and smaller collectives. This symbiosis is a virtuous
feedback loop that only works when properly balanced: neither infinite public
commentary nor 100% corporate control will yield a sustainable Kubernetes for
the long term.

As an engineering manager I am responsible for sending folks straight into the
high-stakes, uncertain front lines of open source development. I am always
trying to connect new folks with seasoned folks, and there is no healthier
community than this one in terms of contributor-maintainer accessibility. I am
very committed to the work to maintain this critical superpower in our
community, and will challenge all of us to recognize and invest in all of our
people, both new and expert!
