## SIGS

- **SIG Apps**: Co-Chair and Technical Lead (TL)
  - Maintainer (approver) of the core Workloads API (Deployment, ReplicaSet, StatefulSet, DaemonSet, etc.)
  - Maintainer (approver) of [Agent Sandbox](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/agent-sandbox), SIG Apps subproject
- **SIG Architecture**: Founder and lead of AI Conformance subproject
- **KubeCon + CloudNativeCon**: Co-Chair Emeritus (2018–2019)

## What I have done

I’ve been part of the Kubernetes community since before 1.0 in 2015. Much of my early upstream work focused on architecting the core **Workloads API** (Deployment, ReplicaSet, StatefulSet, DaemonSet, etc.), designing rollout behavior, unifying controller mechanics, and bringing them from early concepts to GA.

Since then, I served as **KubeCon Co-Chair (2018–2019)** and have led **SIG Apps** as **Co-Chair and TL since 2019**. Alongside ongoing maintenance of the now-mature Workloads API, I'm currently driving new SIG Apps initiatives for emerging workload patterns, most notably the [**Agent Sandbox**](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/agent-sandbox) subproject.

Additionally, I founded and lead the **AI Conformance** program within **SIG Architecture**, establishing cloud-native standards for running AI workloads reliably on Kubernetes, which I demoed during **keynotes at [KubeCon NA 2025](https://youtu.be/cQvtT2vRhok?si=XRetx_UFtSkofdzj&t=893) and [KubeCon EU 2026](https://youtu.be/GkeNG3wxUfY?si=cjmjKeJFUiP1A3s2&t=1999)**. In 2025, I was honored to receive the [CNCF Chop Wood Carry Water Award](https://www.cncf.io/announcements/2025/11/12/cncf-honors-innovators-and-defenders-with-2025-community-awards-at-kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/) in recognition of my long-term commitment to project health and community sustainability.

## What I'll do

I care deeply about keeping the Kubernetes community healthy, sustainable, and productive as we navigate new technical shifts like AI. If elected, my focus will be on enabling cross-SIG initiatives by establishing clear technical boundaries and shared standards, streamlining our community processes to keep them easy to navigate, and ensuring we sustain and support the maintainers who keep everything running.
