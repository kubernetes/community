## What I have done

I’ve been involved in containers and Kubernetes since 2014, contributing directly to SIG Contributor Experience (ContribEx) Communications since 2020. I’ve served as [Comms subproject Co-Lead since 2022](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/commit/f8a1459cc6787a3ef6bb912abd6de7e2b87ec525) and [SIG ContribEx Co-Chair since 2023](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/commit/36e9091305c2af6e14ce3b28adc8d84860e769ce). I was awarded the ["Chop Wood, Carry Water" award in 2023](https://www.cncf.io/announcements/2023/11/08/cloud-native-computing-foundation-announces-2023-community-awards-winners/) and have been a [CNCF Ambassador](https://www.cncf.io/people/ambassadors/?p=kaslin-fields&_sf_s=kaslin+fields) since 2019.

Key accomplishments include:
- **Social Media & Communications:** [Led project social media since 2020](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/commit/ba8cbf2b64f063dc1f8f6fdb5e6006cb0bb3f00e), managing account takeovers across platforms including LinkedIn and the 300k+ follower [Kubernetes.io](http://Kubernetes.io) X account, while onboarding new leads.
- **New Contributor Orientation:** Created the [Kubernetes New Contributor Orientation (NCO) program](https://www.kubernetes.dev/docs/orientation/) in 2024, reaching over 200 live participants and ~6k YouTube views, fostering a sustainable pipeline of active contributors.
- **Community Leadership:** Facilitated weekly comms meetings for 4 years, mentoring contributors into leadership roles across Kubernetes and the CNCF.
