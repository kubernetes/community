# Results of the 2026 Steering Committee Election

- Number of seats open: 3 (2 year term)
- Number of eligible voters: 1547
- Number of votes cast: 308
- Turnout: 19.91%

1. [Michael McCune](https://github.com/elmiko)

4. [Kaslin Fields](https://github.com/kaslin)
